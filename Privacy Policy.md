# Voltexia Hosting — Privacy Policy
## Last Updated: 09-16-2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Voltexia Hosting (“the Company,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information relating to Users (“you,” “your,” “Client”) who access or utilize our Services, including game hosting, VPS hosting, and the Official Discord Server.

By using our Platform or Services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

# 1. Information We Collect

## 1.1 Account Information
We collect the following information when you create an account or purchase Services:
- Email address  
- Payment confirmation details (non‑sensitive; we do not store full card numbers)  
- Billing history  
- Service configuration details (server IDs, resource allocations, IP assignments)

## 1.2 Service Usage Information
To operate and maintain hosting environments, we may collect:
- CPU, RAM, disk, and network usage metrics  
- System logs generated by your VPS or game server  
- Security‑related events (e.g., DDoS patterns, port scans, abnormal resource spikes)  
- Crash reports or automated diagnostic data

## 1.3 Communication Records
We may retain:
- Support tickets  
- Live chat transcripts  
- Discord moderation logs  
- Abuse reports  
- Appeal submissions

## 1.4 Optional Public Information
Users may choose to make certain information public, including:
- Public server listings  
- Community profiles  
- Reseller branding or company name  
- Custom domain associations

By default, **all VPS and hosting environments are private**, unless the User explicitly opts to make information public.

# 2. VPS Privacy & Reseller Privacy

## 2.1 VPS Data Privacy
Voltexia Hosting does **not** access the contents of your VPS, game server files, databases, or stored data unless:
- You grant explicit permission for support purposes, **or**
- Access is required to investigate a severe security threat, abuse incident, or legal request.

We do **not** inspect:
- Private files  
- Databases  
- Application data  
- User content  
- Backups  
- Reseller client data  

## 2.2 Reseller Privacy
If you resell our VPS or hosting Services:
- Your client identities remain private.
- We do not contact your clients directly.
- We do not disclose your reseller status unless required by law or abuse investigation.
- Your VPS environments remain isolated from other Users.

Resellers may choose to:
- Operate anonymously  
- Operate under a brand name  
- Publicly identify themselves  

This choice is entirely optional and controlled by the reseller.

## 2.3 VPS Isolation & Security
Each VPS is isolated using industry‑standard virtualization technologies. We do not:
- Access your VPS console  
- Read your VPS filesystem  
- Monitor your internal application data  

We only collect **resource metrics** necessary for stability and abuse prevention.

# 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
- Provide and maintain hosting Services  
- Monitor system stability and prevent resource abuse  
- Detect security threats, DDoS attacks, or malicious activity  
- Process payments and manage billing  
- Respond to support requests  
- Enforce Terms of Service  
- Comply with legal obligations  

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade User data.

# 4. Logging & Monitoring Practices

## 4.1 System Logs
We maintain limited system logs for:
- Security monitoring  
- Abuse detection  
- Infrastructure performance  
- Troubleshooting  

These logs may include:
- IP addresses  
- Port activity  
- Resource usage  
- Error messages  

We do **not** log:
- User passwords  
- Private VPS content  
- Application‑level data  
- Personal files stored inside your VPS

## 4.2 Discord Logs
For moderation and safety, we may retain:
- Message logs related to rule violations  
- Ban records  
- Appeal records  
- Staff notes  

We do not log private Discord DMs.

# 5. Data Sharing

We may share limited information only when necessary:
- **Payment processors** (for billing verification)  
- **Security partners** (for DDoS mitigation)  
- **Law enforcement** (only with valid legal request)  
- **Infrastructure providers** (for server operation)

We do not share:
- VPS contents  
- Reseller client identities  
- Private user data  
- Support ticket content (unless legally required)

# 6. Data Security

We implement:
- Network isolation  
- Firewall protection  
- DDoS mitigation  
- Access control policies  
- Encryption for sensitive data  
- Regular security audits  

No system is completely immune to risk, but we take reasonable measures to protect User data.

# 7. User Rights

Users may:
- Request deletion of their account  
- Request access to stored personal information  
- Request correction of inaccurate information  
- Request clarification on data handling practices  

Requests may be submitted through our support portal.

# 8. Data Retention

We retain:
- Account information while Services are active  
- Billing records as required by law  
- Security logs for up to 90 days  
- Abuse investigation records for up to 180 days  
- Deleted account data for up to 30 days (for recovery purposes)

VPS data is deleted immediately upon service termination.

These third parties have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their practices.

Our Services are not intended for individuals under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.
